Glossary

Previous topic: Rules And Dates

Activities Of Daily Living

Basic self-care tasks such as bathing, dressing and eating.

Encounter Data

Records of services delivered through managed care plans.

Ex Parte Verification

Confirming eligibility from data the state already holds, without contacting the member.

Self-Declaration

A member's own statement that they qualify, given without documents.

Stable Recovery

Five or more years in recovery from a substance use disorder.

Vacatur

A court order that cancels part or all of a rule.
